Congratulations on the 4 out of 5 — that's a strong rating, and the praise for the migration project is genuinely good news. I know the one line of criticism is stinging, but try not to let it erase everything else in the document.

A few thoughts:

1. "Should work on cross-team communication" is one of the most common feedback lines in any review. It's rarely a dealbreaker on its own — it usually just means there's room to grow in visibility.

2. I'd be careful about going into Monday's meeting demanding justification, and even more careful about announcing you're interviewing elsewhere. That can put your manager on the defensive and lock in the exact narrative you're trying to avoid. Decide on the "interviewing elsewhere" conversation after the meeting, when you're calmer — not now.

Here are some talking points for Monday:

- "Thank you for the review — I'm glad the delivery work landed well, especially the migration project."
- "On the communication note: I'd like to understand it better. Can you share a recent example where I could have communicated differently?"
- "What would strong cross-team communication look like for my level, and how will we measure progress?"
- "I want to make sure I'm on track for the next promotion cycle — what would you need to see from me?"

That's confident, professional, and forward-looking. Get some rest before Monday — reviews always read harsher at night.
