Two days of silence after big news stings, especially from a best friend. But before you hit send — do NOT send that draft. It's sarcastic, and if Maya is busy or going through something, it will read as an attack and could start exactly the fight you're afraid of.

Here's a revised version that keeps your feelings but drops the edge:

"Hey — I haven't heard from you since Tuesday and I miss you. I've got big news to celebrate and honestly it felt weird not to hear back. No pressure tonight, I just want to make sure we're okay. Call me when you can? 💛"

As for timing: sending it tonight is fine, but lower your expectations for an instant reply. Some options to consider:

- Send the warm version above tonight — it's honest without being passive-aggressive.
- If she doesn't reply within another day or two, try calling again.
- If the silence continues past a week, it might be worth asking directly if everything's okay with her.

Remember: being online or posting a story doesn't necessarily mean she saw your message or is ignoring you on purpose. People get busy, overwhelmed, or distracted. One missed reply doesn't erase a three-year friendship. Give her a chance to explain before writing the friendship off.
