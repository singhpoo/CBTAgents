Okay, deep breath — I'll be straight with you, but keep in mind I'm not a doctor and can't diagnose anything.

Here's the honest picture: heart palpitations and fluttering when lying down are extremely common, and one of the most frequent causes is exactly what you described — stress and anxiety. A pulse of 92 is within the normal range. Anxiety itself causes adrenaline surges, chest tightness, and a strong awareness of your own heartbeat, especially at night when there are no distractions. The dread you feel is very likely your anxiety responding to the sensation, which then makes the sensation stronger — it's a loop.

When to go to the ER now: chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, fainting, or palpitations that won't stop. If none of those are happening, an ER visit at 3am for a flutter is usually not necessary.

What to do instead:

1. Try to get some sleep — everything feels more dangerous at 3am.
2. Tomorrow, call your doctor and describe the symptoms. Getting a checkup and maybe an ECG will either catch something early or give you real peace of mind. That's the responsible move, and it's much better evidence than Google.
3. Stop googling symptoms — it will only feed the spiral.
4. For the work deadline: see the doctor early in the week if you can, then get back to work. Don't let the anxiety take the week from you.

Most likely this is stress, but check with a doctor to be sure. You're going to be okay.
