Okay, first: do NOT send a text at 2am. Nothing good gets decided at 2am, and waking up to a heavy "we need to talk" text will make anyone defensive. Wait until morning.

Second, take a breath. Quiet evenings usually mean tired evenings. He said he was tired, he went to bed early — that's a complete explanation on its own. Before assuming he's angry at you, consider the boring possibilities: a stressful week at work, poor sleep, something he's dealing with that has nothing to do with you.

Your draft text has a problem: it assumes he's angry at you and pre-apologizes for something you can't even name. That can actually create a problem where none exists — he'll start wondering what you think he's mad about.

Here's a better version to send (or better, say in person tomorrow):

"Hey — you've seemed a little far away this week. Not accusing you of anything, I just miss you. Is everything okay? Work stuff, or something else? I'm here if you want to talk."

Or even simpler, in person over coffee: "You've been quiet this week — how are you actually doing?"

The difference: it opens the door without confessing to a crime. If something IS bothering him about you, this gives him an easy opening to say it. If it's work stress, you've shown care instead of adding pressure.

So: sleep on it, send nothing tonight, and have a low-pressure conversation tomorrow. You'll handle it better with rest, and so will he.
