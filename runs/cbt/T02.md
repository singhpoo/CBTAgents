I can see why that hour stung — let's slow it down, because the review you received and the review you're experiencing are not the same document.

**Hot thought:** "One sentence erased two years of good work — and this is why I wasn't promoted."

**Distortions at work:** Mental filter — you've read it nine times and your eyes go straight to the one critical line while the rest blurs; you're reviewing the review through that line. Disqualifying the positive — "consistently strong" and the migration praise are being actively discounted as if they don't count. Mind reading — "now I know why" you weren't promoted assumes the review contains an explanation it doesn't actually state.

**Evidence for the hot thought:** The criticism is real and written down. The promotion didn't happen. Those are facts.

**Evidence against:** A 4/5 with "consistently strong delivery" is not the document of someone whose work has been erased — it's the document of someone with a named, specific growth area.
